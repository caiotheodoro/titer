Six headline measurements of commercial people-search products. Five turned out to be artefacts of the instrument, not findings about any vendor. Every one was caught by reading the rows a provider returned — never by reading a summary statistic.

That ratio is the result. The surviving measurements are below, and they matter less than it does.

Why the ratio is the finding

Published comparisons put the same B2B data vendor at 42.4% in one study and 78% in another. Neither publishes a methodology. A gap that size does not come from vendors differing — it comes from harnesses differing. This is a worked example of how.

Calibration

Confidence is pinned at the ceiling

686 of 750 answers (91.5%) sit in the 0.9–1.0 confidence bin, at mean confidence 0.987 against accuracy 0.889. A ten-point overconfidence gap in the bin holding nearly all the mass — and it does not discriminate there. ECE 0.1153.

The fall after three years is most likely our oracle ageing out: executives leave public companies and SEC stops seeing them.

The retraction record2 formal

R001 — the Ploid arm

Three runs, all void. Every failure was a defect in our harness, so no number about Ploid is claimed anywhere. The credits ran out before a clean run existed.

R002 — the raw expertise rates

Negatives built from a top-N summary rather than an exhaustive record. 13.3% were actually true — the same magnitude as the effect being measured.

The retraction machinery — the log, the leak probe, the integrity probe, the claim gates — was committed before any measurement existed. That ordering is checkable in the git history, and it is the only reason five artefacts were caught rather than published.

Limits

One provider. Every surviving number describes Exa; nothing here supports a claim about the category. The surviving 18% may be prompt interpretation — the confirmatory run removed contaminated negatives but cannot remove adjacent ones. Publication record is not expertise: engineers, practising clinicians and lawyers leave no bibliographic trace and are invisible here. Nothing is trained.
